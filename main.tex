\section{Research Profile}
I develop \textbf{representation learning and structured machine learning methods for therapeutic response modeling} across molecular, cellular, and patient contexts. My work connects interpretable graph models, pharmacogenomics, perturbation modeling, and biomedical reasoning, with an emphasis on methods that remain useful under heterogeneous evidence and biological context shifts.

\section{Research Directions}
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}>{\bfseries\color{navy}}p{1.75in}X@{}}
Representation learning & Learning robust molecular, cellular, and patient-state representations that preserve treatment-relevant variation and generalize across biological contexts.\\[3pt]
Perturbation / response modeling & Modeling how therapeutic or genetic interventions change biological state, with emphasis on distributional response, uncertainty, and out-of-context generalization.\\[3pt]
Structured / causal modeling & Incorporating biological graphs, mechanistic structure, and causal assumptions to improve interpretability, intervention reasoning, and transport across contexts.
\end{tabularx}
